# Philippines Exam Retake Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-04-15  
**Geo:** Philippines  
**Contact:** Marlon Luo (can raise and approve LRT requests)  
**Scope:** For learners who failed their first exam attempt and need to retake

---

## Overview

This policy applies to learners who have **failed their first certification exam attempt** and wish to take a **second attempt (retake)**. The company will consider reimbursement for the retake exam ONLY if specific approval and performance conditions are met.

---

## CRITICAL: Pre-Payment Requirements for Retake

### ⚠️ BEFORE Paying for the Retake Exam

1. **LRT Request MUST be raised and approved via 3-layer approval**
   - Marlon can raise and process this request
   - This approval is mandatory for any potential reimbursement eligibility
   - **NO exceptions** - bypassing this step will result in **automatic reimbursement rejection**

2. **Wait for all email approvals** before proceeding
   - Do NOT book/buy/pay for the retake exam without complete approval chain
   - Wait for Marlon's explicit go-signal

---

## Retake Process (After LRT Approval)

### Step 1: Pay Out of Pocket for Retake
- **You must initially pay for the retake exam voucher using personal funds** (own credit card, etc.)
- This is your financial responsibility for the second attempt
- Only proceed after receiving all email approvals

### Step 2: Take the Retake Exam
- Complete the certification exam (second attempt)

### Step 3: Reimbursement Eligibility (ONLY if Passed)

✅ **If you PASS the retake:**
- You can file for reimbursement of the retake exam cost
- Submit reimbursement request after receiving passing results

❌ **If you FAIL the retake:**
- **NO reimbursement available**
- The retake exam cost remains your personal financial responsibility
- You bear the full financial risk of the second attempt

---

## Important Warnings

⚠️ **DO NOT BOOK/BUY/PAY for the retake exam without all email approvals in place**

⚠️ **Wait for Marlon's go-signal to avoid claim rejection due to bypass**

⚠️ **No LRT approval = No reimbursement eligibility** (even if you pass the retake)

⚠️ **Financial Risk:** If you fail the retake, you will NOT be reimbursed - you pay the full cost
