## Notes

- Content, descriptions, and difficulty are unchanged — only names changed.
- Existing certifications are grandfathered and mapped to new titles automatically.
- The CSV credential report (updated June 2026) already reflects new titles.
- EX180 was retired and replaced by EX188. Both qualify as DO288 prerequisites.
