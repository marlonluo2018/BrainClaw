# FutureNow Quarterly Reimbursement

**Effective:** 2026-04-02
**Geo:** China
**Contact:** Yi Kuan Chiew (yikuan.chiew@ibm.com) - Finance & Operations - L&K (GCG)

---

## When This Applies

Each quarter, learner names who completed training/certifications need to be submitted for reimbursement through the FutureNow Center China process.

---

## Steps

1. **Wait for quarterly notification email** from Yi Kuan Chiew
2. **Access the reimbursement list** via link provided in the notification email
3. **Enter learner names** who completed training/certifications in the current quarter
4. **Choose submission method:**
   - Small lists (3-4 learners): Enter names directly into the reimbursement table
   - Larger groups (5+): Create a separate file for the learner list, attach or link to the main list
5. **Submit before end of quarter**

---

## Key Rules

- Quarterly reimbursement lists are recreated at the beginning of each quarter
- Covers training and certification expenses for FutureNow Center China
- Part of L&K (GCG) Enterprise Learning Operations and Administration (ELOA)
- Coordinate with Finance & Operations team for any questions
