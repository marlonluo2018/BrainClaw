# Offcycle Budget Approval

**Effective:** 2026-05-21
**Geo:** China

---

## When This Applies

Any training budget request that falls outside the normal quarterly planning cycle (i.e., not pre-approved in the original Q budget submission).

---

## Steps

1. **Get offcycle approval from Janice** — Email JIANG YU WU (wujyu@cn.ibm.com) with:
   - Training name
   - Headcount
   - Budget amount (USD)
   - Budget source (EPD numbers)
   - CC: HONG YANG (hyhong@cn.ibm.com)

2. **Wait for Janice's approval** — Must be received before proceeding to step 3

3. **Create EPD for George's approval** — Email George Varghese with:
   - Training details
   - Budget source and amount
   - CC: Alponsa, Janice (wujyu@cn.ibm.com), HONG YANG (hyhong@cn.ibm.com)

4. **Assign LDM via Jibu** — Only after EPD is created

---

## Key Rules

- **Sequence is mandatory:** Janice offcycle approval → George EPD approval → Jibu LDM assignment
- **Never skip step 1** — George's EPD cannot be created without Janice's prior offcycle approval
- **Budget source required** — Always include EPD numbers for the funding source
