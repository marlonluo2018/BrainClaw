# Exam Retake Approval

**Effective:** 2026-04-15
**Geo:** Philippines
**Contact:** Marlon Luo (can raise and approve LRT requests)
**Scope:** Learners who failed first exam attempt and need to retake

---

## When This Applies

A learner has failed their first certification exam attempt and wishes to take a second attempt (retake). The company will consider reimbursement ONLY if specific approval and performance conditions are met.

---

## Steps

1. **Raise LRT request** — Marlon raises and processes LRT request (mandatory 3-layer approval)
2. **Wait for all email approvals** — Do NOT proceed without complete approval chain; wait for Marlon's explicit go-signal
3. **Learner pays out of pocket** — Learner pays for retake exam voucher using personal funds (own credit card)
4. **Learner takes retake exam** — Complete the certification exam (second attempt)
5. **Reimbursement determination:**
   - PASS → Learner files for reimbursement of retake exam cost
   - FAIL → No reimbursement; cost remains learner's personal financial responsibility

---

## Key Rules

- **LRT approval is MANDATORY before payment** — No LRT approval = no reimbursement eligibility (even if pass)
- **Never skip the approval step** — Bypassing results in automatic reimbursement rejection
- **Financial risk is on the learner** — If they fail the retake, they pay the full cost
- **Wait for go-signal** — Do NOT book/buy/pay for retake without all email approvals in place
